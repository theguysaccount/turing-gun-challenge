# Product: Turing.gun (human or AI?)

**User.** Anyone curious whether they can still tell people from AI: students, teachers, writers, people who read reviews and messages all day. Secondary: AI watchers who share "it fooled me" moments.

**Painful job.** Deciding how much to trust what you read when anything could be AI-written, with no reliable way to check.

**Promise.** Three minutes, ten real comparisons, your score, and the tells to look for next time.

**Wedge.** The score is the share ("I spotted the AI 7/10 times. Can you?"), and the challenge link replays the same rounds so friends compete fairly. The guide page answers "how to tell if something was written by AI".

**Why now.** Text from new models is hard to tell from human writing, and the question comes up daily. Openly licensed, pre-ChatGPT-style human writing (Dolly, 2023) gives an honest baseline.

**Activation metric.** `game_complete`. Loop: challenge links opened per completion.

**First revenue event.** A sponsored round (`SPONSOR_URL`), for example an AI lab funding a prize round with published rules.

**What v1 deliberately won't do.**
- Chat with strangers who don't know an AI is involved.
- Promise a prize nobody has funded.
- Pretend detection is reliable: the guide says no sign is proof.
