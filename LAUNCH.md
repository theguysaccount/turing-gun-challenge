# Launch and distribution

Live since 2026-10-25 at https://theguysaccount.github.io/turing-gun-challenge/. Machine channels only.

| Channel | How |
|---|---|
| Share loop | The score is a challenge link (same rounds, shows the sender's score on arrival); buttons for text, WhatsApp, Bluesky, native share and copy. |
| Search | Home ("human or AI quiz", "can you spot AI writing", "AI or human test"), the guide ("how to tell if something was written by AI", "signs of AI writing"), structured data, share cards. |
| IndexNow | Every deploy. |
| GitHub | Public repo, description, topics, README. |

## The first 1,000 users (machine plan)

Scores invite competition: each finisher who shares brings friends into the same rounds. The guide page catches the steady search demand around spotting AI text and funnels to the game. Measure once analytics are on (OPERATIONS.md key 5).
